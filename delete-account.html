Delete your Famfinder account

App: Famfinder · Developer: Logicstack LLC

You can permanently delete your Famfinder account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what gets deleted, and what is retained.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Famfinder app on your device and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete in the dialog that appears.

Your account is removed immediately. You will be signed out of all devices.

Option 2 — Email request

If you can no longer sign in to the app or have already uninstalled it, email us and we will delete the account on your behalf.

Email: kernelkrafttech@gmail.com
Subject: Famfinder account deletion request
Include: the email address associated with your Famfinder account.

We aim to process email requests within 7 business days and will reply once the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

  • Your Famfinder profile (display name, email, recovery email).
  • Your last-known and historical location data shared with circles.
  • Your saved Place Alerts and the per-user opt-in settings tied to them.
  • Your membership in any circles you joined.
  • Push notification tokens associated with your devices.
  • Locally cached data on your device (cleared when you uninstall the app).

What is retained, and for how long

  • Anonymous diagnostic and crash logs — retained up to 90 days for product analytics. These cannot be tied back to your account after deletion.
  • Records required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations (e.g. Google Play purchase receipts for any Famfinder Pro subscription) — retained for the period required by applicable law, typically 7 years for financial records.

Effect on circles and other members

If you owned a circle, the circle remains for the other members but your profile, marker, and any Place Alerts you created will be removed.

If you only joined a circle owned by someone else, you will simply be removed from the member list.

Subscriptions

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active Famfinder Pro subscription. To stop billing, cancel the subscription separately in Google Play → Subscriptions before deleting your account.
